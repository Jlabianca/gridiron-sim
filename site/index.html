How to read this

Every game with sportsbook lines is simulated 10,000 times and compared with the best price across major US books. The bar is a mini chart, not a control: the green (or red) fill is the model's chance and the yellow ▼ marks the book's implied chance. Edge is the gap in percentage points. EV is the expected profit per $1 if the model is right. Huge gaps usually mean the model is missing news (an injury, a role change), so treat them as things to check, not bets.

How has the model done?

Every game's projection is saved before kickoff and graded once the final score is in. Picks are the model's side against the closing spread and total, at standard −110 pricing (you need 52.4% to break even). "Strong" means the model gave its side at least 55%.

Cumulative units (ATS + totals, 1 unit per pick at −110)

Backfilled games (weeks before the scorecard existed) were projected with models rebuilt using only the data available before that week. Live picks are snapshots saved by the daily update before kickoff.

Players marked out by the injury report (Out/Doubtful), or no longer active on the roster, start unchecked. Toggle anyone in or out — their carries and targets are re-split among the rest. Changes apply to both tabs.
